{% translate "The words, in plain language" %}

{% translate "Bill day (statement day)" %}
{% translate "The day each month the bank adds everything up into a bill. Printed on your card bill." %}
{% translate "Pay-by day (due date)" %}
{% translate "The last day to pay that bill. Pay all of it by then and you pay no interest." %}
{% translate "The bill" %}
{% translate "Everything new since the last bill, plus whatever was left unpaid from it." %}
{% translate "Minimum payment" %}
{% translate "The least you must pay by the pay-by day to avoid a late fee — usually 5% of the bill, at least ৳500. Interest still runs on the rest." %}
{% translate "Interest" %}
{% translate "What the bank charges, about 20–27% a year, when a bill isn't paid in full." %}
{% translate "Card fee (to bKash / Nagad)" %}
{% translate "What the card's bank takes when you send money from the card to a wallet: 0%, 1%, 1.5%, 2%… It is added to what you owe on the card." %}
{% translate "Mobile banking fee" %}
{% translate "What bKash, Nagad or Rocket take when you pay a card bill from them, or send money to a bank. It comes out of the wallet." %}
{% translate "EMI" %}
{% translate "A bill or a purchase split into equal monthly payments, one on each bill. Often 0% for a few months; sometimes with interest or a fee." %}
{% translate "Limit · Available" %}
{% translate "The most the card lets you owe, and how much of it is left. Keeping under 30% used looks better to banks." %}
{% translate "Match the bank" %}
{% translate "If the app's amount differs from the bank's, type the bank's number on the card's page. The difference is saved as a correction." %}

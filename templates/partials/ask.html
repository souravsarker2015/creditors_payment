{% load i18n %}{% comment %}The app's own question box, in place of the browser's confirm() and prompt(), for every part of the app. A form or submit button with data-confirm="Delete this? What happens next." asks before it's sent; pages ask with FT.ask(). data-confirm-after says what a delete leaves you with: "back" (it can be brought back) or "gone" (it can't). data-confirm-check adds what's attached to this very record, from the server (apps/core/delete_check.py). After a yes the box stays until the next page comes: behind it the old page still shows a record that's already gone.{% endcomment %}
